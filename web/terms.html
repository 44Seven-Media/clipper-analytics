Clipper Network analytics · 44Seven Media · Last updated November 5, 2026

Clipper Network is an internal analytics tool operated by 44Seven Media for tracking social media accounts that 44Seven Media owns or manages.

Who may use it

Only people invited by 44Seven Media may sign in. Accounts may be connected only by someone authorized to manage them.

What it does

When an account is connected, the tool reads that account's public profile and performance statistics through the platform's official API, once a day, and shows them to the team. It does not post, comment, message, follow or change anything on any connected account.
