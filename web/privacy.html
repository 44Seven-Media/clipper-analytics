Privacy Policy

Clipper Network analytics · 44Seven Media · Last updated November 5, 2026

Clipper Network is an internal analytics tool run by 44Seven Media. It tracks the performance of social media accounts that 44Seven Media owns or manages. It is not offered to the public, and only invited team members can sign in.

What we collect

  • From connected social accounts (Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts, TikTok, YouTube, X), only with the account owner's permission: the account's name, username, profile picture and profile link; follower, following, like and post counts; and, for recent posts, their title or caption, link, thumbnail, publish date, and view, like, comment and share counts.
  • Access tokens that the platform issues when an account is connected, so the tool can refresh these numbers once a day.
  • Team members' sign-in details: email address and a password, which is stored as a secure hash by our sign-in provider.

We do not collect private messages, contacts, payment information, or data about the people who follow or view the connected accounts.

How we use it

Only to show 44Seven Media's team how its own accounts are growing: follower counts over time, views, and top-performing posts. We do not sell, rent or share this data, use it for advertising, or combine it with other data about individuals.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a Supabase database hosted in the United States. Access tokens are readable only by the tool's server-side functions, never by the web page. The web page is served by GitHub Pages.

How long we keep it

Account statistics are kept while the account is tracked. Removing an account in the tool deletes its statistics, posts and access tokens. Tokens are also deleted if the platform revokes them.

Disconnecting and deleting data

An account owner can disconnect at any time by removing the app in the platform's own settings (for example Facebook Business Integrations, TikTok Manage app permissions, Google Account third-party access, or X Connected apps), or by asking us. To have data deleted, see our data deletion instructions.
